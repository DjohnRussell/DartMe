# Privacy Policy for DartMe

Daniel John Russell built the DartMe app as an Open Source app. This SERVICE is provided by Daniel John Russell at no cost and is intended for use as is.

## Information Collection and Use

**DartMe does not collect, store, or share any personal user data.** 

* **Local Storage:** All game scores and player names are stored locally on your device's memory while the app is running. This data is not transmitted to any external servers.
* **Permissions:** The app uses the device's vibration motor (VIBRATE permission) for haptic feedback during use. This does not involve any data collection.
* **Third-Party Services:** The app does not use any third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.
